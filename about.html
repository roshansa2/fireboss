About Fire Boss

A one-point solutions provider for cease-fire protection — trusted by industries, corporates and consumers since 1999.

Who we are

Protection you can rely on

Fire Boss — Cease Fire Protection — introduces itself as a one-point solution for fire protection for industries, corporates and all kinds of consumers, wherever the product is needed. We deal in all types of ISI fire extinguishers as per the requirements of our clients.

The organisation has been active since 1999 and is ISO 9001:2015 certified. Our products are manufactured by S.K Industries Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi, and carry our own ISI mark for IS 15683 portable fire extinguishers.

Our aim is simple: to attain the highest level of customer satisfaction through service — we save lives.
