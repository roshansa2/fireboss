Questions

Frequently asked

Do you supply ISI-marked fire extinguishers?
Yes. Our portable fire extinguishers carry our own ISI mark for IS 15683, and we deal in all types of ISI extinguishers as per client requirements.
What does the “free 12 services in a year” include?
It is our promise to keep your fire-safety equipment serviced through the year. Message us on WhatsApp and we will explain how it applies to your requirement.
Can you help with Fire NOC and system design?
Yes — Fire Consultancy (Design & NOC) is one of our core services. We guide you through system design and the NOC formalities.
Do you refill and maintain extinguishers bought elsewhere?
We specialise in refilling and maintenance as a contractor and can service all types of fire extinguishers under an AMC. Share the details and we will confirm.
Do you offer CCTV and security systems too?
Yes. We supply dome, bullet and PTZ CCTV cameras with recorders alongside our fire-safety range.
